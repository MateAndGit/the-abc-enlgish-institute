About Babel Academy of English

Babel Academy of English, founded in 2017, is an independent school with two locations in the heart of Dublin City.

Recognised as one of Ireland’s top English schools, we’re proud of our strong sense of community, where students feel supported, connected, and genuinely welcomed by the entire team.

You’ll study on Eden Quay, beside the famous O’Connell Bridge, or on Capel Street, just a 5-minute walk away. Being in the city centre means you’re close to everything: historic landmarks, cafes, nightlife, and the iconic Temple Bar, only 2 minutes from our door.

We believe learning English is more than just classroom time. That’s why we offer a fun social programme that helps you explore Dublin, meet new people, and practice English in real-life situations.
